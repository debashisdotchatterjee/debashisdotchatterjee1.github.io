Research identity

Rigorous statistics, designed for real scientific complexity

A research programme connecting probability, uncertainty quantification and modern statistical learning to directional, spatial, temporal and high-dimensional scientific data.

β

Bayesian inference & inverse problems

Bayes factors, inverse regression, posterior consistency, empirical priors, and uncertainty-aware decision frameworks.

↻

Directional & circular statistics

Circular, spherical, and directional models for trajectories, rhythmic phenomena, celestial impacts, migration, and geoscientific data.

∇

Statistical learning & AI

Mechanistically informed graph learning, classification, generalized additive modelling, reinforcement learning, and interpretable predictive systems.

⊕

Interdisciplinary statistics

Astronomy, epidemiology, climate, water, environmental monitoring, morphometrics, optimization, and scientific computing.

Appointments & training

Statistics across theory and application

Assistant Professor in Statistics at Visva-Bharati since July 2021 and Adjunct Fellow at the S. N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences. Academic training includes B.Stat. (2013), M.Stat. (2015), and Ph.D. in Statistics (2022) from the Indian Statistical Institute.

The doctoral thesis, A Brief Treatise on Bayesian Inverse Regression, was supervised by Prof. Saurabh Bhattacharya at ISI.
