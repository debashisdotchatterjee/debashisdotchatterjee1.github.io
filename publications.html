Research programme

Four connected strands

Bayesian foundations

Inverse regression, Bayes factors, posterior convergence, empirical priors, and uncertainty calibration.

Directional statistics

Circular and spherical modelling, mixture structures, directional regression, and rhythmic or trajectory data.

Statistical learning

Graph learning, classification, optimization, reinforcement learning, generalized additive models, and data-driven forecasting.

Scientific applications

Astronomy, epidemiology, climate, ecology, water, morphometrics, biomedicine, and operations research.
